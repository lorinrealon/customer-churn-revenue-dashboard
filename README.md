# Telco Churn & Revenue-at-Risk Dashboard

An interactive dashboard that estimates the probability of customers canceling their service based on account and billing information, subscribed services, and customer demographics. It helps businesses build proactive retention and revenue-protection strategies.

---

## Business Problem

Customer churn directly reduces recurring revenue. This dashboard helps teams identify key churn factors, quantify revenue at risk, and target retention offers rather than guessing which changes or incentives will keep customers from leaving.

---

## Key Findings
- Month-to-month customers carry substantially higher churn risk than customers on one-year or two-year contracts.
- New customers in their first year face the highest churn exposure, while longer-tenured customers are much more likely to stay.
- Fiber optic customers and customers without tech support face noticeably higher churn risk than DSL users, non-internet customers, or those with tech support.
- In the model and what-if simulator, higher monthly charges can lower predicted churn risk because higher billing tiers often coincide with longer-tenured, bundled accounts and higher cumulative charges (TotalCharges), rather than price increases themselves reducing churn.
